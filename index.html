Early Internet Spaces

Poptropica

Poptropica was an online adventure game made in Adobe Flash, meaning sadly it is now lost. The game was made up of over 50 islands, or levels. Each island had a distinct theme and storyline with characters to interact with and platforming puzzles to solve. While some islands were remade for Steam and HTML5, many of them are lost forever. You can technically play on Cool Math Games, but the feeling is much different than it used to be.

The first island I ever completed was 24 Carrot Island, with some help from my cousin who introduced me to the game.

The story of this island was that a small town was noticing carrots and random teenagers going missing, so our character explores underground and finds the lair of Dr. Hare, a mad scientist planning on using carrots as fuel for his "Rabbot" rocketship.

Some of my favorite parts of this island were:

  • Navigating the underground vent maze
  • Platforming through the factory to avoid dangerous machinery
  • Sabotaging the rocket by failing a Space Invaders-type minigame
